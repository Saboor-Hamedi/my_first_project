# MindForge Basics

Working with your notes in MindForge is straightforward, fast, and completely reliable.

---

## Creating & Writing Notes

Press Ctrl+N anywhere in the app to create a new note. MindForge immediately opens a blank canvas ready for typing.

### Automatic Saving
You never have to worry about losing your work. MindForge automatically saves your writing to the local SQLite database after 1.2 seconds of typing idle time.
- Press Ctrl+S anytime for an instant manual save confirmation.
- The status dock at the bottom confirms when your note is safely stored.
- An asterisk indicator appears next to the title whenever unsaved changes exist.

---

## Managing Your Notes Library

The sidebar provides instant access to your notes:
- Toggle Sidebar: Press Ctrl+B or click the sidebar toggle icon.
- Load a Note: Click any note in the list to load it immediately into the editor.
- Rename Note: Press Ctrl+R to open the quick rename dialog. Type your new title and press Enter.
- Delete Note: Press Ctrl+Shift+D (or type :d / :delete in command mode) to open the confirmation modal. Confirm with Enter or dismiss with Esc.

---

## Fast Fuzzy Note Search

Press Ctrl+P to open the lightning-fast fuzzy search overlay:
- Start typing any word or phrase from a note title or body content.
- Use Up and Down arrow keys to navigate the filtered results.
- Press Enter to open the selected note immediately.
- Press Esc to close search and return to your note.

---

## Return to Notes from Docs

When browsing documentation guides:
- Click the "Return to Notes" button at the bottom of the doc sidebar.
- Or type :editor in the bottom command bar to return immediately.
- Your note content and cursor position remain preserved exactly where you left off.

---

## Switching Between Hybrid and Vim Modes

MindForge lets you seamlessly switch between standard modern IDE input and full Vim modal editing:
- **Turn Vim ON**: Type `:vim on` or select **VIM** in Settings (`Ctrl + ,` -> Editor Mode).
- **Turn Vim OFF**: Type `:vim off` or select **HYBRID** in Settings.
- **Toggle Anytime**: Press `:` and type `:vim` to quickly toggle between them.
