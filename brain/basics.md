# MindForge Basics

Working with your notes in MindForge is straightforward, fast, and reliable.

## Creating a Note

Press Ctrl+N anywhere in the app to create a new note. MindForge immediately opens a blank page ready for typing.

## Writing and Automatic Saving

You never have to worry about losing your work. MindForge automatically saves your writing to your local database as you type.

- Press Ctrl+S anytime for an instant manual save confirmation.
- The status bar at the bottom will confirm your changes are saved.

## Managing Your Notes

The sidebar gives you full access to all your notes:

- Open or Close Sidebar: Press Ctrl+B or click the sidebar toggle.
- Switch Notes: Click on any note in the list to load it instantly into the editor.
- Rename Note: Press Ctrl+R to open the quick rename popup, type your new title, and press Enter.
- Delete Note: Press Ctrl+Shift+D to open the safe deletion prompt. Confirm with Enter or cancel with Esc.

## Finding Notes Quickly

Press Ctrl+P to open the fast search window. Type any word from a note title or content to find it instantly, use Up and Down arrows to select, and press Enter to open it.
