# Customization & Features

Make MindForge your own with built-in personalization and productivity tools.

## Custom Themes & Colors

Open Settings with Ctrl+, to personalize MindForge's visual theme:
- Choose from modern accent colors like Amber, Indigo, Rose, Teal, or Emerald.
- Adjust editor font size and line spacing for maximum reading comfort.

## Typing Audio Feedback

MindForge can play satisfying mechanical keyboard sounds as you type:
- Enable or disable audio anytime in the Settings menu (Ctrl+,).
- Volume level can be fine-tuned to your preference.

## Writing Statistics

Switch to the Stats view from the sidebar to track your writing momentum:
- View your total word count and notes created.
- See your writing activity for today and previous days.

## Offline Reliability

MindForge stores everything in a local SQLite database directly on your device.
- No third-party servers.
- No tracking or telemetry.
- 100% functional without an internet connection.
